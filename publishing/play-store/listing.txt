FULL DESCRIPTION
-----------------
Amma is a simple way to stay close to family, built for parents and
grandparents who'd rather talk than type.

Just tap the mic and talk, like you're calling your child. Amma listens,
and replies in a warm, natural voice — even in your child's own cloned
voice, if they choose to set that up.

FEATURES

• Tap and talk — no typing, no complicated menus
• Replies in a familiar voice, not a robotic assistant
• Ask Amma to call or message someone by name, and it's handled
• Cast videos or music to your TV just by asking
• Works in Tamil and English

Amma is designed to be simple enough for anyone, especially those who
find typing or navigating apps difficult. One button. Just talk.

PRIVACY

Voice cloning is entirely optional and requires explicit consent, which
can be withdrawn at any time. Phone numbers never leave your device —
only the app's ability to look them up locally is used to place calls or
send messages. See our privacy policy for full details:
https://dhana-siva.github.io/amma-cast-receiver/privacy.html
