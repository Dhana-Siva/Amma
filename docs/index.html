Amma Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026  ·  Applies to Amma on iOS and Android, for families using either the Amma or Appa persona.

Plain-language summary

The short version

  • Every time you tap and talk, what you say is sent to Anthropic (the AI that writes Amma's replies) and to ElevenLabs (which turns your speech into text, and text into Amma's spoken voice). This is core to how Amma works, and happens on every message.
  • We keep your family's setup info (names, optional phone number, language) and your conversation history on our own server, so Amma can remember context between messages.
  • Recording a voice sample so Amma can reply in a familiar voice is entirely optional, asked for separately, and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Contact numbers stay on your phone. Only the name you say out loud — never the number itself — is sent anywhere.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What happens when you talk to Amma

Talking to Amma is the app's core feature, and it works by sending your voice and words to two outside companies that specialize in AI. Here's exactly what goes where:

DataSent toWhyKept afterward?
Your voice recording ElevenLabs Transcribed into text ("speech-to-text"), using your family's language setting to transcribe accurately No — the recording is discarded once we get the text back
The text of what you said Anthropic Given to Claude, the AI model that writes Amma's reply, along with recent conversation history for context Our server — saved as conversation history, so Amma remembers earlier messages
Amma's written reply ElevenLabs Converted into spoken audio ("text-to-speech") so Amma can talk back No — the audio is streamed to your device and not stored by us

Anthropic's and ElevenLabs' own privacy practices govern how they handle what we send them. You can read Anthropic's privacy policy and ElevenLabs' privacy policy for their own details.

Your family's account information

When you set up Amma, the parent's name, the child's name, an optional phone number for the child, and a language preference are stored on our server. This lets Amma greet the right people, call the right number when asked, and reply in the right language — it isn't shared with Anthropic or ElevenLabs beyond what's needed for the specific reply being generated (for example, the name used in a greeting).

Optional: a voice sample, so Amma sounds familiar

If you choose to, you can record about 30 seconds of your voice so Amma can reply using a custom voice instead of a default one. This is entirely separate from the core Talk feature described above:

  • It's off unless you explicitly turn on the consent toggle in Voice Setup.
  • Your recording is uploaded to ElevenLabs, which uses it to create a synthetic voice.
  • You can withdraw this consent at any time from the same screen, which stops the custom voice from being used going forward.

Calling family by name

Amma can look up a phone number from your phone's Contacts app so you can say a name — like "call Geetha" — instead of a number. This lookup happens entirely on your device. Only the name you say is ever sent to Anthropic as part of your message; the phone numbers in your Contacts never leave your phone.

What we don't collect

Location Financial information Browsing history Health data Advertising identifiers

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Voice cloning consent can be granted or withdrawn any time from Voice Setup.
  • Contacts access can be granted or revoked any time from your device's Settings, or declined during onboarding.
  • Deleting your data: to request deletion of your family's conversation history and account information from our server, contact us using the details below.

Families with children

Amma is designed to be used together, with a parent setting up the family and a child using the Talk feature to stay in touch. The same data practices described above apply regardless of the child's age. If you're a parent or guardian with questions about a child's data, or want it removed, contact us below — we'll act on that request directly.

Changes to this policy

If how Amma handles data changes — for example, adding a new third-party service — we'll update this page and the date at the top, and call out material changes in the app itself.

Questions about this policy or your family's data? Contact us at privacy@ammaapp.example.
